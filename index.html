Three questions, every lot

Most listings answer only the second one, and put it in the header in large type.

Is it the right molecule?

Mass spectrometry weighs what is in the vial and compares it to the expected mass. Purity says nothing about identity — a vial can be 99% pure and 99% the wrong thing. This test is the one that gets skipped.

How much of the vial is that molecule?

Reversed-phase HPLC at 214 nm, reported as area percent. We publish the method and the wavelength alongside the number, because the same sample gives different figures under different conditions and an unlabelled percentage is not comparable to anything.

If it is a blend, is the ratio real?

A three-compound vial needs a fourth test. Fill weight tells you the total; it does not tell you the proportion. Every blend in the register carries a ratio verification, not an assumed split.
